An AI operations team for every growing company.

NowAI will take on the orders, invoices, calls and payments that reach a company every day. People keep the decisions that matter, and our fee will follow the money we find.

01Mission and vision

From six busy doors to one calm brief.

Growing companies run on chat apps, accounting software and paper. NowAI will give them an operations team that works day and night, and a clear view of where the money is.

02What NowAI will do

Work arrives through six doors. Money leaks in the gaps.

NowAI will read all six doors, in Azerbaijani and Russian. It will finish the routine work behind them: orders, invoice checks, reminders and entries for the books.

The six doors, and what slips through each

  • WhatsApp

    Orders as voice notes and photos of lists, often after hours.

  • Phone

    Questions, complaints and orders, at the busiest hours.

  • Email

    Supplier invoices, quotes and reconciliation acts.

  • Paper

    Delivery notes and signed acts, retyped by hand.

  • Bank

    Statement lines matched to invoices, one by one.

  • State portals

    e-qaimə lists, tax files and EMAS contract data.

Why start in Azerbaijan

  • WhatsApp first

    Shops, patients and dealers order and ask on WhatsApp, often by voice note. NowAI would answer there, not in a new app.

  • 1C and e-qaimə

    Books are often kept in 1C (estimate). Input VAT depends on a proper e-qaimə (reported). NowAI would work from these files.

  • Two languages, often in one message

    Customers mix Azerbaijani and Russian, in text and voice. NowAI would read both (planned). Azerbaijani voice is the main technical risk.

Who it is for. Mid-size and large private firms with a lot of incoming work. Large banks and large telecom operators are not first targets: some already run their own AI assistants (reported). Who we would not serve

03How it will work

From one week of data to roles that work every day

Every client will follow the same four steps. Nothing will go live without the client's approval.

  1. 01

    Discovery Sprint

    Two weeks on one week of the client's own data, under a written agreement, ending in a Money Map.

  2. 02

    Replay

    The first two roles will run on past weeks, to show what they would have caught.

  3. 03

    Review-only, then rules

    Roles will go live with every action waiting at the Gate. Rules written with the client then let routine work pass.

  4. 04

    Paid on measured value

    A monthly statement by bucket, with the evidence under each number. The fee will follow it.

04Principles

Six rules we will hold ourselves to

They shape the product, the price and this site.

  1. 01

    Clear fees and measured outcomes

    A platform fee a month, plus a share of the value we measure. If nothing is measured, only the platform fee.

    In practiceA monthly statement by bucket, with the evidence under each number (target).

  2. 02

    A person approves what matters

    Postings, payments, large credit notes and orders over a credit limit would wait at the Gate (the approval queue).

    In practiceNowAI would never hold ASAN İmza or client money.

  3. 03

    Show first, then sell

    People rarely know what they want until they see it. So every client would start with a paid two-week Discovery Sprint on one week of its own data.

    In practiceThe sprint ends in a Money Map, with evidence under every number (target).

  4. 04

    Work inside the client's own systems

    No new app for shops or staff. NowAI would answer on the client's own channels and work from its 1C exports, bank files and e-qaimə files (planned).

    In practiceWriting back into 1C would use import files, to be confirmed with 1C dealers (unknown).

  5. 05

    Growth without new hires, not layoffs

    The roles would take the typing, checking and chasing. A company could grow without hiring more people for that work.

    In practiceHours saved are shown, not charged.

  6. 06

    Label every number

    Every figure says where it comes from: model, target, estimate or a named source. When we do not know, we write unknown.

    In practiceWhat each label means

05Roadmap

Ninety days to answer one question

Does a company pay for the money we find? Five workstreams will answer it, and day 90 will decide what comes next. target

Week 123456789101112

  1. Weeks 1-4

    15 owner interviews

    Owners and finance directors at distributors, chains and clinics. Done when there are 15 write-ups: where work arrives, what slips, what it is worth, who signs.

  2. Weeks 1-4

    Discovery tooling

    Readers for a WhatsApp export, an inbox, a 1C export and a bank statement, and a Money Map template. Done when it runs on our own sample data.

  3. Weeks 3-8

    Three Discovery Sprints with design partners

    Free or discounted, under a written data agreement. Done when there are three Money Maps with evidence for every number.

  4. Weeks 6-12

    Two roles in replay at two companies

    Likely Payables Check and Collections. Done when the buckets are measured against the Money Map.

  5. Weeks 1-12

    Legal answers in writing

    Data leaving Azerbaijan, AI voice calls and call recording, WhatsApp consent, payment links. Done when a lawyer has written a note on each.

  6. Week 12

    The day-90 decision

    Go, change sector, or stop, by the rules below.

Three possible outcomes on day 90

  • Go

    At least 2 of 3 sprints find value of at least 3× the proposed fee, and at least one company signs a paid contract. target

  • Change sector

    The sprints find value, but nobody signs. target

  • Stop

    The sprints find less than 2× the fee. target

The 3× test counts value as the model does: the four buckets plus Hours (shown, not charged). Value includes Hours (shown, not charged); the fee is charged only on the four buckets.
