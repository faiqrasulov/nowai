Our operating principles

In practice

  1. Outcomes with evidence

    We agree with the client on what to measure before work starts.

    Each outcome on the monthly statement comes with evidence.

  2. A person approves what matters

    Postings, payments, large credit notes and orders over the credit limit wait at the Gate for approval.

    Your company keeps control of its funds and ASAN İmza.

  3. Validate workflows before deployment

    Every client starts with a two-week Discovery Sprint using one week of its own data.

    The Money Map shows where value could come from and gives the evidence behind each figure.

  4. Work in the systems you use

    NowAI answers on your existing channels and works from 1C exports, bank files and e-qaimə invoices.

    NowAI prepares 1C import files for someone on your team to review and post.

  5. Increase operating capacity

    NowAI takes on typing, checking and follow-ups so your team can handle more work.

    We report hours saved separately from financial outcomes.

  6. Show the basis for every figure

    We distinguish estimates, targets, reported figures and verified outcomes. A measured outcome needs evidence from a sprint.

Work comes in from many places.

What NowAI does

Work comes in from many places.

NowAI reads incoming work in Azerbaijani and Russian and handles routine orders, invoice checks, reminders and accounting entries.

What arrives and where work gets stuck

  • WhatsApp

    Orders as voice notes and photos of lists, often after hours.

  • Phone

    Questions, complaints and orders, at the busiest hours.

  • Email

    Supplier invoices, quotes and reconciliation acts.

  • Paper

    Delivery notes and signed acts, retyped by hand.

  • Bank

    Statement lines matched to invoices, one by one.

  • State portals

    e-qaimə lists, tax files and EMAS contract data.

Why start in Azerbaijan

  • WhatsApp and voice notes

    Customers already send orders and questions on WhatsApp, often by voice note. NowAI answers on the same channel.

  • 1C and e-qaimə invoices

    Many businesses keep their books in 1C (estimate). NowAI uses 1C exports and e-qaimə invoices to check supplier documents.

  • Azerbaijani and Russian

    Customers often mix the two languages in text and voice. NowAI handles both and sends unclear voice notes to the Gate for review.

Who it’s for

Mid-sized and large private firms with a high volume of orders, invoices or customer requests.

How it works

From one week of data to roles that work every day

Every client follows the same four steps. Nothing goes live without the client's approval.

  1. 01

    Discovery Sprint

    Two weeks on one week of the client's own data, under a written agreement, ending in a Money Map.

  2. 02

    Replay

    The first two roles run on past weeks, to show what they would have caught.

  3. 03

    Review-only, then rules

    Roles go live with every action waiting at the Gate. Rules written with the client then let routine work pass.

  4. 04

    Review the measured outcomes

    A monthly statement shows what changed and the evidence behind each figure.

    The day-90 decision

Three possible outcomes on day 90

  • Go

    At least two of three sprints meet the target for measurable value, and at least one company signs a contract. target

  • Change sector

    The sprints find value, but nobody signs. target

  • Stop

    The sprints fall below the minimum target for measurable value. target

Deployment partner requirements

Deployment partner requirements

Each deployment has specific system, hosting and compliance requirements. These are the provider categories NowAI evaluates against those requirements.

  • A licensed payment provider

    For payment links in reminders, after legal review. Clients keep control of their funds.

  • Subscriber-billing vendors

    For regional internet providers and dealer networks: balances, outages and payments. Needed before any telecom work.

    Telecommunications

  • Loan and insurance software vendors

    For insurers' agencies, microfinance and leasing firms: instalments, arrears and claim files.

    Financial services

  • 1C dealers

    For import routes, so approved entries can go back into 1C. Route and terms to be agreed.

  • A voice and telephony provider

    For calls in Azerbaijani and Russian, chosen on Azerbaijani voice quality and cost.

  • A hosting provider

    Where client data lives, chosen on a lawyer's written answer about data leaving Azerbaijan.
