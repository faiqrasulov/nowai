Layer 6: Human approval queue

Human approval queue

The safety gate. Every proposed entry, reminder or payment waits here and shows its evidence, its confidence and what changed. The accountant approves, edits or rejects. Rejections feed client memory. Nothing reaches the ledger or leaves to an outside system without going through it.

What we would use
A queue table plus accountant app screens. Later, auto-approve only for rules the accountant switches on per client.

Five facts that shaped this drawing

  • An accountant approves every entry. Azerbaijani law requires a certified chief accountant for firms that need one, and software cannot sign or file. NowAI never holds a client's ASAN İmza.
  • NowAI never holds client money. The payment partner route is planned and legally unconfirmed. Epoint and Payriff are the candidates.
  • State systems are files at launch. No public e-taxes, e-qaimə or EMAS API was found, so the accountant handles these files.
  • Hosting is undecided. No rule requiring servers in Azerbaijan was found, but a lawyer must confirm, because Claude API calls send client documents abroad.
  • Accuracy is untested. Accuracy on real Azerbaijani receipts is unmeasured. No benchmark exists, so a pilot on 200+ real receipts comes first.

What this assumes

  • 60 documents per client per month. The real number is unknown.
  • 80 percent clean at $0.002, 20 percent hard at $0.06. That blends to $0.0136.
  • 10 WhatsApp utility messages and $0.30 of other model use.
  • 1 USD = 1.70 AZN. Hosting, the WhatsApp provider fee, partner fees and salaries are left out.

Accuracy on real Azerbaijani receipts is untested, so the 20 percent is a guess. The bigger cost is people. Model fees are small next to accountant time and salaries.

Two numbers still need reconciling. The financial model puts the direct cost per client at about 5.7 AZN a month. This design estimate is about 2.3 AZN. The model uses a cautious reading cost and counts more cost lines. Until the two are reconciled, treat 2.3 AZN as the low case and 5.7 AZN as the safe case.

Three routes, honestly

1. Regional money flow

Main route. Long shot.

Needs 180,000+ firms across the region, earning on money that moves through a licensed partner.

2. Higher-price markets: UAE, Saudi Arabia

A plausible few million dollars a year

Bookkeeping costs 15 to 70 times more there. It needs Arabic, a certified e-invoicing provider, local partners and local hosting, and Wafeq, Qoyod, Zoho and OCTA are ahead. It does not reach $1B on its own.

3. Strategic sale

The most likely good outcome

Buyers such as Visma and Xero have paid for this kind of company. At Conta Azul's price per client: about $7M at 2,500 clients, about $72M at 24,000 clients.
