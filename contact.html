Introduction brief

What to include in your introduction

Describe the company and workflow in general terms. Use the agreed data-sharing process for company records.

  1. 1

    Industry and company size

    For example: a distributor, a pharmacy chain, a clinic group.

  2. 2

    Highest-volume channel

    WhatsApp, phone, email, paper, bank or state portals.

  3. 3

    Workflow to assess

    Describe what comes in, who checks it and what the team produces.

Please do not send

  • Customer lists, phone numbers or chats
  • Invoices, contracts or bank statements
  • Staff, patient or student data
  • Passwords, logins or ASAN İmza details
