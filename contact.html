01How to reach us

Tell the person who shared this site with you.

They will pass your message to the founder. The reply comes back the same way.

  • Why an introduction

    NowAI starts with conversations, not forms. An introduction from someone you know takes your message straight to the founder.

  • Who we would like to hear from

    • Owners and finance directors
    • Distributors and wholesalers
    • Retail and pharmacy chains
    • Private clinic groups

  • What happens next

    If your company fits the first focus, the founder may invite you to one of the first 15 owner interviews (target).

    No pitch. Three questions: where does work arrive, what slips through, and what is that worth?

    Which industries come first

02What to say

What helps in a first message

Three things are enough. Please send no customer data.

  1. 1

    Your industry, and roughly how big the company is

    For example: a distributor, a pharmacy chain, a clinic group.

  2. 2

    Which door is busiest

    WhatsApp, phone, email, paper, bank or state portals.

  3. 3

    Whether you would consider a Discovery Sprint

    Two weeks on one week of your data, 5,000 AZN (target).

Please do not send

  • Customer lists, phone numbers or chats
  • Invoices, contracts or bank statements
  • Staff, patient or student data
  • Passwords, logins or ASAN İmza details

Before any sprint, data is shared only under a written data agreement, signed first. How the agreement would work

One week of your work. Two weeks later, your Money Map.
