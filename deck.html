NowAI
Clients send photos. The entries are prepared for you. The accountant approves.
NowAI: the AI back office for Azerbaijani small firms.
Investor deck, November 2026. Draft: no product, customers or revenue yet.
Summary
Books is the door and money flow is the business; Azerbaijan alone is too small.
What the newest $1B companies show
Accountants are a proven channel in rich markets. Small home markets reached $1B by owning the money flow or selling globally. Most of these companies disclose no revenue.
What NowAI does
An AI junior for accounting practices. Clients send photos on WhatsApp, the AI prepares checked entries for 1C, and the accountant approves each one. Cash comes second.
What the numbers say, honestly
Azerbaijan alone is not enough: the realistic ceiling is about $2.4M a year, and the base case runs out of cash around month 45. $1B takes 180,000 to 330,000 paying firms.
What we need
We need 1.5M AZN, about $0.9M. It is a plan. No money has been raised. 1.5M AZN carries the Base case to month 45. A further 0.31M AZN is needed by month 60 and $10M by year 5 for step 3. The first 90 days test whether practices pay.
So what
Nothing is built, no practice has been asked and there is no revenue. The deck is an argument and an arithmetic check. It reports no results.
Source: NowAI financial model, 6 Nov 2026 (model); Crunchbase News, 10 Aug 2026; company figures from Bloomberg, TechCrunch and Fortune (reported). USD at 1.7 AZN per USD.

The product
The AI junior would be built, tested, run and improved with the accountant in the loop.
1. Build (planned)
The accountant would describe how a client works and add past files. The agent would draft rules for review.
Sample moment (sample data) Nümunə Market is set up from 6,480 past documents: 62 suppliers, 14 rules.
2. Test (planned)
Every change would run against a test set grown from accountant corrections before it goes live.
Sample moment (sample data) Replay of October: 397 of 412 match, 96.4%. One new rule lifts it to 98.5%.
3. Run (planned)
A client photo arrives on WhatsApp. The agent would propose an entry and a reason. The accountant would approve, edit or reject.
Sample moment (sample data) An invoice for 240.72 AZN arrives as a photo. The entry waits for one click.
4. Improve (planned)
Each edit would teach client memory. The agent would flag what nobody has tagged, such as overdue invoices.
Sample moment (sample data) NowAI flags 18,420 AZN overdue from 6 customers, 61% of the bills due by the 20th.
So what
The accountant is in the loop at every step, so a wrong entry is caught before it reaches the books. The demo uses synthetic data only.
Source: NowAI plan and backend design, 6 Nov 2026 (design, not built). Sample moments use sample data from the demo, which is synthetic; no real client or document has been used.
