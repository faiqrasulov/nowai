The AI back office for Azerbaijani small firms, run by their accountant.

Accountants spend their month typing receipts and chasing rule changes. Small firms cannot afford more staff.

Build

Hand over last year's files. NowAI sets up the client.

  • Learns from your filesReads the cash book, bank statements, 1C exports and your voice notes.
  • Writes plain rulesEach client gets rules you can read, change or reject.
  • Checks before going liveIt replays a past month, so you see the result before real books change.

Test

Replay a past month before anything goes live.

  • Scored on your own workEvery document is compared with the entry your accountant made.
  • Every difference explainedSee why each one failed, with the documents behind it.
  • Fix it in plain wordsApprove a rule, rerun the month and see the new score.

Run

Every day, from photo to approved entry.

  • Reads the documentEach field carries a confidence dot, so you check only what needs checking.
  • Proposes the entryDebit and credit lines, with the suppliers it learned in Build.
  • Matches the bankLinks the payment to the invoice and checks the e-qaimə. Accountants match the delivery note (qara qaimə) to the e-qaimə by hand today; NowAI would do it line by line.

Improve

It reads across all your clients and speaks up.

  • Finds what nobody taggedLooks across every client for patterns a busy team misses.
  • Answers in moneyAsk how big it is and get a figure in your team chat.
  • Prepares the workIt drafts the reminders. You review them before anything is sent.

Cash

The owner sees what is coming, in plain numbers.

  • What is owed and what is dueOne card with the money coming in and the bills going out.
  • The day cash runs outA daily balance line shows the first day below zero.
  • One tap to actThe owner sends the reminders the accountant reviewed.
