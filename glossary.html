01Our words

NowAI's own terms

Fourteen terms, written the same way on every page.

NowAI Core

The shared layer every role runs on: channels, reading in Azerbaijani and Russian, company memory, connectors, the Gate, the audit trail and measurement. Designed once, shared by every role.

The seven roles

Each role does one kind of daily work, on its own channels. We say roles, not agents: a role has a job, and a person approves its risky steps.

The Gate

The approval queue. Anything risky waits there for a person, with its evidence: posting to the books, sending money, large credit notes, orders over a credit limit, messages outside the rules.

What waits at the Gate, and why
Discovery Sprint

The paid start: two weeks on one week of the client's own data.

Money Map

What a sprint ends in: where work waits and money leaks, in AZN, with the evidence under every number. The client keeps it either way.

The four buckets

The four kinds of value the outcome fee is charged on.

  • Extra salesorders that would otherwise be missed, at their margin.
  • Cash in soonermoney in earlier, counted as the cost of waiting.
  • Losses avoidedbad debt that does not happen.
  • Leaks recoveredovercharges, duplicate bills and input VAT kept.
How each bucket is measured
Hours (shown, not charged)

Staff time saved, written in AZN. The owner sees it, but no fee is charged on it.

Measured value

Value in the four buckets, checked against the client's own records each month. The outcome fee is a share of it.

Platform fee

The fixed monthly part of the price. If nothing is measured, it is all the client pays.

Replay

Running a role, or a new rule, on the client's past weeks to see what it would have done. It comes before anything goes live.

Review-only mode

The first live stage. A role works on live messages, but every action waits at the Gate for a person.

Deployment engineer

The NowAI person who sets up a client: doors, rules and replay tests. The plan assumes one for every 8 live clients (estimate).

Door

A way work reaches a company: WhatsApp, phone, email, paper, the bank, state portals. Money leaks in the gaps between doors.

The brief

Owner's Brief's short message to each person in charge, at the time they choose: orders, cash, problems found, decisions needed and money found this week.

03Local terms

Words from Azerbaijani business life

Systems and rules a company in Azerbaijan meets every week, and how NowAI works with each.

1C

Accounting and business software that many firms in Azerbaijan keep their books in.

With NowAI: NowAI reads 1C exports first. Writing back uses import files, prepared for a person to post.

e-qaimə

The electronic tax invoice. It is made in the tax portal or the offline tool, signed with ASAN İmza and uploaded (reported).

With NowAI: Payables Check matches each purchase invoice to its e-qaimə. It never issues one.

ƏDV

Value added tax. The rate is 18% (verified).

With NowAI: Payables Check flags input ƏDV at risk when an e-qaimə is missing.

VÖEN

The taxpayer identification number. Every company and registered entrepreneur has one.

With NowAI: Payables Check uses it to match an invoice to the right supplier.

ASAN İmza

The state mobile electronic signature. Managers and accountants sign filings and e-qaimə with it.

With NowAI: NowAI never holds a client's ASAN İmza. The accountant signs.

EMAS

The state system for electronic labour contracts. A contract, a change or an ending takes effect only once e-signed there (reported).

With NowAI: People Desk prepares the data for a person to enter in EMAS.

AZN

The Azerbaijani manat. Amounts on this site are in AZN unless they show a dollar sign.

With NowAI: Every amount is in AZN, written the way each language writes it: 1,248.00 AZN in English, 1 248,00 AZN in Azerbaijani.
