02Where work arrives

Six doors in. Money leaks between them.

Orders, invoices, calls and payments reach a company through six doors. Nobody sees all six at once, so money slips through the gaps.

  • WhatsApp

    What arrives
    Voice notes, photos of order lists, promises to pay.
    Where it leaks
    An order sent at 22:00 waits until morning, or goes to a rival.

  • Phone

    What arrives
    Orders, complaints and “where is my delivery?”
    Where it leaks
    Calls missed at busy hours are never logged.

  • Email

    What arrives
    Supplier invoices, price lists, reconciliation acts.
    Where it leaks
    A price that drifts from the contract gets paid anyway.

  • Paper

    What arrives
    Delivery notes, handwritten lists, signed acts.
    Where it leaks
    Everything is retyped by hand. Short deliveries slip through.

  • Bank

    What arrives
    Statement files and payments with unclear references.
    Where it leaks
    Shops that paid still get chased. Late ones get missed.

  • State portals

    What arrives
    e-qaimə and EMAS.
    Where it leaks
    A missing e-qaimə loses input VAT. A missing EMAS contract risks a fine.

NowAI would read all six, in Azerbaijani and Russian. What NowAI Core would hold

04The Gate (the approval queue)

Nothing risky moves until a person clicks Approve.

Every risky action would wait with its evidence. A person approves, edits or rejects it. Otherwise it does not happen.

By rules you approve

  • Orders within a shop's credit limit.
  • Reminders under rules your finance manager approved.
  • Small credit notes, under a limit you set.
  • A routine e-qaimə reminder to a supplier, tied to one invoice.

Waits for a person, with the evidence

  • Posting to the books.
  • Any payment. NowAI would only prepare it; a person releases it.
  • Large credit notes.
  • Orders over a credit limit.
  • Any message outside your rules.
  • Every new rule, after a replay on past weeks.

Never

  • Hold your money.
  • Hold your ASAN İmza.
  • Threaten or pressure your customers.
  • Change a rule without a person's yes.

08Questions

Straight answers

If your question is missing, tell the person who shared this site with you.

When could we start?

With a Discovery Sprint. The first three places will go to design partners in our first-focus industries, free or at a discount (target). How the sprint would work

Who is it for?

Mid-size and large private companies with a lot of incoming work. First focus: distributors, retail and pharmacy chains, private clinics. Not large banks, large telecom operators or micro firms. All industries

Will it replace my staff?

No. It would do the routine work, and your people would approve what matters. The aim is growth without new hires, not layoffs.

What if it gets something wrong?

Anything risky would wait at the Gate for a person. New rules would be replayed on past weeks first. Each role would start in review-only mode, with every action waiting for approval.

How would the fee be measured?

A 3,500 AZN platform fee plus 20% of the value we measure in four buckets (target). Hours are shown, not charged. Each month would come with a statement and its evidence. How each bucket would be counted

Where would our data go?

Only the data you choose, under a written agreement signed before the sprint. Where it is stored would be decided after a lawyer's written answer on data leaving Azerbaijan.
