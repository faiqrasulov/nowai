One moment, up close

A lead comes in after closing. The answer comes from the dealer's own price list. The sales team confirms the test drive.

What it would check before replying

  • The dealer's own price list, from its latest export
  • Free test-drive slots for Saturday
  • Whether the car is in stock or on order
  • The dealer's rule: discounts and trade-ins go to a person

NowAI would never agree a price, a discount or a loan. It would answer from the dealer's own list and pass the rest to the sales team.

02Leads at night, parts by voice note

Where work waits and money leaks

Buyers may write when the showroom is closed. Workshops may order parts in a hurry. Both sides may want an answer within minutes.

Drawn from desk research; a Discovery Sprint would test it on your own data. estimate

  1. 01

    Leads can wait until morning

    Questions from listing sites and Instagram can arrive after closing. By morning, the buyer may already have asked other dealers.

  2. 02

    Follow-up can stop after a day or two

    A lead who does not reply can be forgotten. Nobody may write again a week later.

  3. 03

    Service calls missed

    Owners may call about bookings, repair status and parts while advisors are busy with cars in the workshop.

  4. 04

    Past buyers may drift to other garages

    If nobody reminds owners when service is due, the work can go elsewhere.

  5. 05

    Parts ordered by voice note, on credit

    Workshops may order parts on WhatsApp and by phone. Stock and credit may be checked by hand, or not at all.

  6. 06

    Parts invoices and dealer data keyed by hand

    Importer and supplier invoices may be checked against orders by hand. Dealer-system data may be retyped into 1C.

05The Gate

What would always wait for a person

Anything risky would wait at the Gate (the approval queue) for the sales manager, the service manager, the parts manager, the credit controller or the chief accountant. Prices, discounts and credit always stay with a person.

By rules you approve

  • Answers taken from the dealer's own price list
  • Reminders from approved templates, each tied to one invoice
  • Repair status read from the service export

Waits for a person, with the evidence

  • Every test drive and service slot, confirmed by staff
  • Discounts, price exceptions and trade-in values
  • Late cars, and goodwill credits above a limit
  • Parts orders over a credit limit
  • Every posting to 1C
  • Supplier emails and every payment release
  • Payment plans and disputed invoices

Never

  • Agree a price or a discount
  • Approve a loan, a lease or an instalment plan
  • Hold buyer money
  • Hold your ASAN İmza

07Before you ask

Questions

Which dealer systems would it work with?

Work would start from the dealer's own exports. Which dealer systems are common here is one of the first things a sprint would learn.

Which languages would it use?

Azerbaijani and Russian, in text and voice, often mixed in one message (planned). Azerbaijani voice is the hardest part, so anything unclear would wait at the Gate for a person.

What if it gets something wrong?

Anything risky would wait at the Gate for a person. New rules would be replayed on past weeks first. Each role would start in review-only mode, with every action waiting for approval.

Where would our data go?

Only the data you choose, under a written agreement signed before the sprint. Where it is stored would be decided after a lawyer's written answer on data leaving Azerbaijan.
