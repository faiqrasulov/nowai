Implementation

Reorder prompts. Sales Desk will remind a shop of its usual order, from an approved template, if the shop opted in. Not in the first roles.

Approval rules

Each exception goes to the person who owns the decision, with its supporting records. Finance handles credit limits, Purchasing reviews supplier replies, and the chief accountant approves 1C postings.

Runs under approved rules

  • Orders within the shop's credit limit
  • Reminders from approved templates, each tied to one invoice
  • Credit notes below the amount you set
  • A reminder to a supplier about one missing e-qaimə

Requires human approval

  • Orders over a credit limit
  • Credit notes above the rule
  • Emails to suppliers about overcharges
  • Every 1C posting, in one batch for the chief accountant
  • Any message outside the approved templates

Never

  • Hold your ASAN İmza
  • Hold or move your money
  • Sign or issue an e-qaimə
  • Change a price or a credit limit by itself

How control works

Operational bottlenecks

Distributor teams receive orders, invoices and payments across several channels. Delays or mismatched records can affect sales, collections and supplier payments.

Industry research informs these examples. A Discovery Sprint checks them against your records. estimate

  1. After-hours orders

    Orders sent after hours can be delayed or missed when staff re-enter voice notes and photos in 1C the next day.

  2. Credit-limit checks

    A delayed balance check can allow an order to ship after the customer has exceeded their credit limit.

  3. Overdue invoice follow-up

    Ad hoc follow-up tends to prioritise the largest balances. Smaller invoices can remain overdue without a reminder.

  4. Unmatched payments

    Transfers with incomplete references can stay unmatched, leading to reminders on invoices the shop has already paid.

  5. Supplier price discrepancies

    Invoices arrive by email, on paper and as WhatsApp photos. A few qəpik more per jar, on thousands of jars, can be paid without a check.

  6. Missing e-qaimə

    Input VAT needs a proper e-qaimə (reported). If nobody tracks which suppliers still owe one, the credit can be lost.

Questions

Before you ask

Questions

Do our shops need a new app?

No. Shops keep writing on WhatsApp and calling as they do now. The replies come under your company's name.

Which languages does it use?

Azerbaijani and Russian, in text and voice, often mixed in one message. Anything unclear waits at the Gate for a person.

What if it gets something wrong?

Anything risky waits at the Gate for a person. New rules are replayed on past weeks first. Each role starts in review-only mode, with every action waiting for approval.

Where does our data go?

Only the data you choose, under a written agreement signed before the sprint. Where it is stored is set out in that agreement.
