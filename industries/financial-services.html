01Three kinds of firm, one pattern

Where work waits and money leaks

Claims, loans and leases all depend on reminders, documents and matching. We expect much of it is still done by phone and by hand.

Drawn from desk research; a Discovery Sprint would test it on your own data. estimate

Insurers' agencies and brokers

  1. 01

    Claim notices in pieces

    Photos, policy numbers and documents can arrive by phone and WhatsApp, in pieces. Staff then match them to the right claim by hand.

  2. 02

    Status calls and lapsed renewals

    Customers may call again and again to ask where a claim stands. If renewal reminders go late, policies lapse.

Microfinance and non-bank lenders

  1. 03

    Early arrears grow quietly

    Where reminder calls are made by hand, they can be late and uneven. A promise to pay may not be followed up on the day.

  2. 04

    Repayments from many channels

    Branches, terminals, cards and transfers. Each small payment must be matched to the right loan.

Leasing firms

  1. 05

    Instalments and insurance on the asset

    A late instalment can be noticed late. Insurance on a leased car or tractor can lapse unseen.

  2. 06

    End of term and dealer invoices

    Buyout and renewal decisions can come too late. Dealer invoices must match the contract price and the e-qaimə.

03Measured, not assumed

Where the money would come from

The money would sit in instalments paid sooner, arrears that do not roll on, and invoices that match. We would show a figure only once a sprint has measured one firm.

A sprint would measure this industry's value from a company's own data.

  • Cash in sooner

    Instalments and premiums paid sooner. Counted by the days saved.

  • Losses avoided

    Early arrears that would otherwise roll into bad debt, and leased assets left without cover. Counted against the sprint's baseline.

  • Leaks recovered

    Repair-shop, clinic and dealer overcharges, credited back. Counted only when the credit arrives.

  • Extra sales

    Renewals and loan applications completed. Counted only against the sprint's baseline.

  • Hours (shown, not charged)

    Status and balance calls answered, repayments matched. Shown, not charged.

04The Gate

What would always wait for a person

A licensed specialist, a credit officer, the head of credit, the operations manager, the leasing manager or the chief accountant would approve. Regulated decisions would never leave them.

By rules you approve

  • Reminders on approved templates
  • Status and balance answers from the latest export, after an identity check
  • Requests for a missing document

Waits for a person, with the evidence

  • Claim files, for a licensed specialist to decide cover and payout
  • Restructuring, hardship and write-off requests
  • Any change to contract terms
  • Disputes with repair shops, clinics or dealers
  • Every 1C posting
  • Disputed balances and payoff quotes
  • Leased assets without valid cover

Never

  • Deciding a claim or a loan
  • Releasing a payment, or holding or collecting money
  • Threats, or mentioning courts
  • Reminders or arrears messages outside approved templates

05Before any promise

What a sprint would measure, and what we still need to learn

A sprint would measure one week. Two legal questions need answers before any reminder is sent.

What a sprint would measure here

  • Arrears by age, and promises to pay kept
  • Policies and leases that lapsed or ended without a decision
  • Repair-shop, clinic and dealer invoices above the agreed price
  • Staff time on status and payoff calls

What we still need to learn

  • Central Bank rules on how and when lenders may contact borrowers unknown
  • Whether payment links through a licensed provider are allowed unknown
  • Which loan and policy systems these firms use, and what they export unknown
  • The size of the microfinance sector: sources conflict unknown

06Short answers

Questions

Would it decide claims or loans?

No. Claim decisions, payouts, restructuring and write-offs would stay with a licensed person. NowAI would never hold client money.

Which languages would it use?

Azerbaijani and Russian, in text and voice, often mixed in one message (planned). Azerbaijani voice is the hardest part, so anything unclear would wait at the Gate for a person.

What if it gets something wrong?

Anything risky would wait at the Gate for a person. New rules would be replayed on past weeks first. Each role would start in review-only mode, with every action waiting for approval.

Where would our data go?

Only the data you choose, under a written agreement signed before the sprint. Where it is stored would be decided after a lawyer's written answer on data leaving Azerbaijan.
