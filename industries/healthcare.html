02Where patients and money slip away

Where work waits and money leaks

Patients reach a clinic group by phone, WhatsApp and Instagram, in Azerbaijani and Russian. Insurers pay later, and may send back incomplete claims.

Drawn from desk research; a Discovery Sprint would test it on your own data. estimate

  1. 01

    Calls missed at peaks and after hours

    Patients may call when the line is busy or the clinic is closed. Some may book somewhere else.

  2. 02

    No-shows and empty slots

    A patient forgets, or cancels late. The slot can stay empty, and the waiting list may never hear.

  3. 03

    Repeat tests not always booked

    A check-up or a repeat test falls due. Nobody may have time to write to the patient.

  4. 04

    The same questions all day

    Prices, preparation, opening hours, whether results are ready. Answering them one call at a time can take much of the front desk's day.

  5. 05

    Insurer claims sent back for a missing paper

    A claim that goes out without a referral or a signature can come back weeks later. Meanwhile the money waits.

  6. 06

    Consumables and lab invoices

    Supplier prices can drift from the contract. A purchase without an e-qaimə loses its input VAT credit (reported).

05The Gate

What would always wait for a person

Anything risky would wait at the Gate (the approval queue), for the front desk lead, the branch manager, the lab manager or the insurance claims officer, as in the day above. Anything clinical would skip it and go straight to a doctor or nurse.

By rules you approve

  • Offering free slots from the latest schedule export
  • Day-before reminders from approved templates
  • Offering a freed slot to opted-in patients on the waiting list
  • Sending the clinic's own preparation note

Waits for a person, with the evidence

  • Every booking and move, confirmed by the front desk
  • Refunds
  • Discounts outside the price list
  • Every insurer claim submission
  • Supplier emails and every posting to 1C

07Before you ask

Questions

Would it give medical advice?

No. NowAI would never answer clinical questions or explain results. They would go to a doctor or nurse, by rule.

Which languages would it use?

Azerbaijani and Russian, in text and voice, often mixed in one message (planned). Azerbaijani voice is the hardest part, so anything unclear would wait at the Gate for a person.

What if it gets something wrong?

Anything risky would wait at the Gate for a person. New rules would be replayed on past weeks first. Each role would start in review-only mode, with every action waiting for approval.

Where would our data go?

Only the data you choose, under a written agreement signed before the sprint. Where it is stored would be decided after a lawyer's written answer on data leaving Azerbaijan.
