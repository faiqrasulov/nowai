06Plainly

Not first targets

Some firms are too large, some too small, and some are run by the state. We say so before anyone asks.

  • Large banks. One large bank has launched its own AI voice assistants (reported).
  • Large mobile and fixed-line operators. One large mobile operator launched its own Azerbaijani voice assistant in 2023 (reported).
  • State utilities.
  • State bodies.
  • Micro firms and small online-only shops: the price is too high for them.

The two later pages cover the mid-size tier only: regional internet providers and dealer networks, and insurers' agencies, microfinance lenders and leasing firms.

07Four tests

How we sorted them

An industry moves up the list when it passes all four tests. This is our judgement, not a finding from clients (estimate).

  1. 01

    A buyer who owns revenue and cash

    The owner, the CEO or the finance director. They would judge the fee against money found.

  2. 02

    A lot of incoming work

    Orders, invoices, calls and payments every day, through many doors at once.

  3. 03

    1C, e-qaimə and WhatsApp

    The local systems NowAI is designed around. Work would start from files and exports.

  4. 04

    Value of about three times the fee

    Enough money in the four buckets, with Hours counted the way the model counts them (model).

Value includes Hours (shown, not charged); the fee is charged only on the four buckets.
