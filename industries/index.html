Where NowAI fits best

Apply these criteria when assessing a workflow for automation.

  1. 01

    A decision-maker accountable for outcomes

    The owner, CEO or finance director reviews the evidence and decides where to start.

  2. 02

    High volume of routine work

    Orders, invoices, calls and payments arrive through multiple channels every day.

  3. 03

    1C, e-qaimə and WhatsApp

    The local systems NowAI is built around. Work starts from files and exports.

  4. 04

    Measurable operational value

    Measure financial outcomes in the four value categories. Track staff time separately.

Money found counts the four buckets. Staff time saved is shown and not counted.
