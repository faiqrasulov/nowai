02Two sides, both leaking

Where work waits and money leaks

A chain buys from many suppliers and sells to many customers. Money can slip out on the supplier side. Sales can slip away on the customer side.

Drawn from desk research; a Discovery Sprint tests it on your own data. estimate

  1. 01

    Rebates promised, not always received

    Year-end bonuses and promotion allowances may be agreed outside the invoice. Whether they ever arrive can go unchecked.

  2. 02

    Promo prices billed at full price

    The shelf price drops for a promotion, but the supplier's invoice may not. Then the difference gets paid.

  3. 03

    Duplicates and missing e-qaimə

    The same invoice can arrive by email and on paper. A purchase without an e-qaimə loses its input VAT credit (reported).

  4. 04

    Credits for returns that come late, or not at all

    Returned and near-expiry stock goes back to the supplier. The credit note can be promised, then forgotten.

  5. 05

    “Where is my order?” “Is it in stock near me?”

    Customers may ask on WhatsApp, Instagram and the phone, in Azerbaijani and Russian. If nobody answers after hours, the sale can go elsewhere.

  6. 06

    Instalments and cash on delivery, chased by hand

    Some electronics and furniture chains sell on instalments. Some online orders are refused at the door. Both may be followed up by hand, if at all.

05The Gate

What always waits for a person

Anything risky waits at the Gate (the approval queue). In the week above, it waits for the chief accountant, the delivery manager, the online sales manager or the purchasing manager. Refunds above the rule wait for the customer service lead. Questions about medicines do not wait there: they go straight to a pharmacist.

By rules you approve

  • Holding one item for pickup, within the chain's rules
  • Refunds below the amount you set
  • Reminders from approved templates, each tied to one payment
  • A reminder to a supplier about one missing credit note or e-qaimə

Waits for a person, with the evidence

  • Refunds and credits above the rule
  • Supplier dispute emails and debit notes
  • Every posting to 1C
  • Any message outside the approved templates

Never

  • Answer questions about medicines, doses or symptoms
  • Hold customer money or card details
  • Hold your ASAN İmza
  • Change a shelf price

07Before you ask

Questions

Does it answer questions about medicines?

No. Questions about medicines, doses or symptoms go to a pharmacist, by rule. NowAI answers stock, branch and order questions only.

Which languages does it use?

Azerbaijani and Russian, in text and voice, often mixed in one message. Anything unclear waits at the Gate for a person.

What if it gets something wrong?

Anything risky waits at the Gate for a person. New rules are replayed on past weeks first. Each role starts in review-only mode, with every action waiting for approval.

Where does our data go?

Only the data you choose, under a written agreement signed before the sprint. Where it is stored is set out in that agreement.
