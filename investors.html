01Summary

The decision on one page

The business on one page: what we will build, who we sell to, how we price it, and how day 90 decides what comes next.

What we will build
The AI operations team for mid-size and large companies: seven roles on NowAI Core, with a person approving what matters at the Gate (the approval queue).
Sell to
The owner or finance director of distributors, retail and pharmacy chains, and private clinics. Azerbaijan first, then the wider region. Not banks or telcos.
Sell what
Money found: Extra sales, Cash in sooner, Losses avoided, Leaks recovered. Hours are shown, not charged.
Price
A 3,500 AZN platform fee a month plus 20% of measured value (target). On the model's mix it blends to 6,571 AZN a month (model).
Start with
A two-week Discovery Sprint on one week of the client's own data, ending in a Money Map. Two roles would then run in replay on its past weeks. List price 5,000 AZN; the first three, with design partners, free or discounted (target).
Why it can work
The model's distributor gets 22,782 AZN a month of value, 3.2× its fee (7 November model). On the reconciled basis a mature three-role client gets about 2.0× its fee, and NowAI's margin is 67% (model).
Why it might not
Every value driver still has to be measured. On one consistent basis NowAI first turns a profit in month 48 and needs about 4.44M AZN over 84 months. With engineers at 6,000 AZN a month instead of 4,500, there is no break-even inside 84 months (model).
Money
About 350,000 AZN first, for the 90-day test, a second industry and a cash cushion. Then about 942,000 AZN, sized on actual spend (targets, provisional).
Ceiling and stretch
About $7.0M a year from Azerbaijan. $1B needs about 2,160 clients across the region (model).
First 90 days
15 owner interviews, 3 design-partner sprints, 2 roles measured in replay, legal answers in writing (target).
Go
At day 90, at least 2 of 3 sprints find at least 3× the proposed fee, and at least one company signs a paid contract (target).
Change sector
Value is found, but nobody signs. target
Stop
The sprints find less than 2× the fee. target

Value includes Hours (shown, not charged); the fee is charged only on the four buckets.

06Competition

Who else does this work, without names

This site names no companies. These are categories.

  • Global AI-agent companies

    They sell to banks, telcos and government (reported).

  • Local accounting and ERP tools

    Each does part of the back office (reported).

  • In-house staff and outsourcers

    The real alternative today. We would sell growth without new hires, not layoffs.

Five differences

  1. 01

    The tier

    Mid-size and large private firms, below the banks and telcos those companies chase first (estimate).

  2. 02

    Front office and back office together

    From a shop's WhatsApp message to the 1C entry, the bank line and the e-qaimə.

  3. 03

    Local systems

    1C exports, e-qaimə files, EMAS, local bank statements, and Azerbaijani and Russian voice (planned).

  4. 04

    Price

    About 6,600 AZN a month on the model's mix (target). Only the platform fee if nothing is found.

  5. 05

    The sprint

    A paid two-week start that ends in a Money Map, before any contract (target).

We found nobody here joining front office and back office for mid-size firms. That is a search result, not proof.

07Risks

What could go wrong, and what we would watch

Four risks decide the plan: becoming an agency, sales pace, unmeasured value and a larger player moving down-market.

Risks, early signs and what we would do. Early-sign thresholds are targets.
RiskEarly signWhat we would do
Becoming an agencyEach client needs custom code; fewer than 5 clients per deployment engineer.A fixed menu of roles; setup is configuration, not code; clients per engineer tracked every month.
Sales slower than plannedFewer than 2 sprints signed by month 6.Paid sprints after the design partners; narrow to the sector that converts.
Value smaller than modelledMoney Maps below 3× the fee.Price from the measured Money Map; a lower platform fee and a higher outcome share.
No local late-payment dataReceivables in sprints close to terms.Lead with Leaks recovered and Extra sales instead.
A larger player moves down-marketGlobal players pitching distributors and clinics.Speed, local systems, price, and front office through to back office.
Data and lawA lawyer says consent or local hosting is needed.Get the answer before the first sprint; a quote for local hosting.

What would prove us wrong

  • Two of three sprints find less than twice the fee.
  • No company pays for a sprint or a contract by day 90.
  • Companies want a chatbot, not a role that finishes the job.
