01Summary

The decision on one page

The business on one page: what we build, who we sell to, what it is worth to a client, and how day 90 decides what comes next.

What we build
The AI operations team for mid-size and large companies: seven roles on NowAI Core, with a person approving what matters at the Gate (the approval queue).
Sell to
The owner or finance director of distributors, retail and pharmacy chains, and private clinics. Azerbaijan first, then the wider region. Not banks or telcos.
Sell what
Money found: Extra sales, Cash in sooner, Losses avoided, Leaks recovered. Staff time saved is shown, not counted.
Price
Not settled. The plan assumes about 5,200 AZN a month from a client with four roles (assumption). The owner interviews test it before any pilot is offered.
Start with
A two-week Discovery Sprint on one week of the client's own data, ending in a Money Map. Two roles then run in replay on its past weeks. List price 5,000 AZN; the first three, with design partners, free or discounted (target).
Why it can work
In the middle case the example distributor finds about 111,000 AZN a year, 0.4% of its sales; the low case is about 29,000 and the high about 221,000 (estimates; no company has been measured). On the plan's assumptions a client pays about 5,200 AZN a month and costs about 1,800 to run, and the margin at maturity is 67% (estimate).
Why it might not
Every value driver still has to be measured. On one consistent basis NowAI first turns a profit in month 48 and needs about 4.44M AZN over 84 months. With engineers at 6,000 AZN a month instead of 4,500, there is no break-even inside 84 months (estimate).
Money
About 350,000 AZN first, for the 90-day test, a second industry and a cash cushion. Then about 942,000 AZN, sized on actual spend (estimates, provisional).
Ceiling and stretch
About $7.0M a year from Azerbaijan. $1B needs about 2,160 clients across the region (estimate).
First 90 days
15 owner interviews, 3 design-partner sprints, 2 roles measured in replay, legal answers in writing (target).
Go
At day 90, at least 2 of 3 sprints find three times the price NowAI quotes, and at least one company signs a paid contract (target).
Change sector
Value is found, but nobody signs. target
Stop
The sprints find less than twice the price. target

05Weak points

Where the plan is weakest

  • Engineer cost decides the long run. Recruiting evidence in the first three months shows which column applies.
  • In the low case all three example companies find less in a year than the plan assumes a client pays.
  • The model does not raise churn when value is low. Reality would.
  • Onboarding and measurement days have no time logs yet, so the seed round is sized after the pilots, not now.

The ask: about 350,000 AZN first, then about 942,000 AZN (estimates, provisional). The first tranche funds seven months: the 90-day test, a second industry and a cash cushion. The second follows, to month 18, released on the evidence and sized on what was actually spent.

06Competition

Who else does this work, without names

This site names no companies. These are categories.

  • Global AI-agent companies

    They sell to banks, telcos and government (reported).

  • Local accounting and ERP tools

    Each does part of the back office (reported).

  • In-house staff and outsourcers

    The real alternative today. We sell growth without new hires, not layoffs.

Five differences

  1. 01

    The tier

    Mid-size and large private firms, below the banks and telcos those companies chase first (estimate).

  2. 02

    Front office and back office together

    From a shop's WhatsApp message to the 1C entry, the bank line and the e-qaimə.

  3. 03

    Local systems

    1C exports, e-qaimə files, EMAS, local bank statements, and Azerbaijani and Russian voice.

  4. 04

    Price

    About 5,200 AZN a month in the plan's mid case (assumption). Not settled: the owner interviews test it.

  5. 05

    The sprint

    A paid two-week start that ends in a Money Map, before any contract (target).

We found nobody here joining front office and back office for mid-size firms. That is a search result, not proof.

07Risks

What could go wrong, and what we watch

Four risks decide the plan: becoming an agency, sales pace, unmeasured value and a larger player moving down-market.

Risks, early signs and what we do. Early-sign thresholds are targets.

What would prove us wrong

  • Two of three sprints find less than twice the price.
  • No company pays for a sprint or a contract by day 90.
  • Companies want a chatbot, not a role that finishes the job.
