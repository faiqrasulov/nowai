Payables Check

e-qaimə and input VAT

A supplier invoice without its e-qaimə can cost the buyer its input VAT. Here is what the rules say, and what Payables Check would do.

What the rules say

  • Input VAT can be credited only against a proper e-qaimə.

    reported

  • A paper invoice, where an e-invoice was required, gives no credit.

    reported

  • An e-qaimə is made in the tax portal or the offline tool, signed with ASAN İmza and uploaded.

    reported

  • We found no official public API for e-qaimə. Work would start from files.

    reported

  • ƏDV, the value added tax, is 18%.

    verified

What Payables Check would do

  • Match each purchase invoice to its e-qaimə.
  • Flag every invoice that has none, with the ƏDV at risk beside it.
  • Remind the supplier with an approved template, tied to that one invoice. A routine reminder could pass by a rule the client writes.

Payables Check would never sign or issue an e-qaimə. The accountant does that, with their own ASAN İmza.

  1. e-qaimə rules, the portal and the offline tool (reported): Tax service, electronic invoice page
  2. ƏDV rate (verified): Tax summary for Azerbaijan, corporate taxes
  3. Input VAT and paper invoices: a third-party guide, not yet checked against the Tax Code (reported).

How measurement fits the Core

How each bucket would be measured

The fee follows measured value. So each bucket needs a plain rule, and evidence a client can check line by line.

Extra sales

An order taken that would have been missed, such as one sent at night. It counts at the client's margin, not the full sale.

Evidence: the message, the time it arrived and the order in 1C.

Cash in sooner

Money that arrives sooner than it used to. Each day saved counts as the cost of waiting, at 15% a year (estimate).

Evidence: each payment date against its due date and the client's own past average.

Losses avoided

Bad debt that does not happen. It is set against the client's own write-offs in past years.

Evidence: the account, the age of the debt, the reminders sent and the payment that came.

Leaks recovered

Money kept that would have leaked: an overcharge, a duplicate bill, or input VAT lost for want of an e-qaimə.

Evidence: the invoice line, the contract price and the payment that was held.

Hours (shown, not charged)

Staff time saved, written in AZN. It is shown in the brief and the statement. No fee is charged on it.

Evidence: the tasks a role would do, timed against how long a person took in the sprint week.

How the fee would follow

  • Each month would bring a statement: value by bucket, with the evidence under every line (target).

Value includes Hours (shown, not charged); the fee is charged only on the four buckets.

How control would work

What waits at the Gate, and why

Some actions are hard to undo. Those would wait for a person, with the evidence beside them.

  1. Waits for a person

    Posting to the books

    An entry changes the accounts a company reports. The chief accountant would approve entries, often in one batch.

  2. Waits for a person

    Sending money

    A payment is hard to call back. A person in the client's company would release every one.

  3. Waits for a person

    Large credit notes

    A credit note is money given back. Small ones could pass by a rule the client writes. Large ones would wait.

  4. Waits for a person

    Orders over a credit limit

    Credit limits belong to a named person, often the finance manager. An order over a limit would wait for them.

  5. Waits for a person

    Any message outside the rules

    A new kind of reply, a promise, or a price not in the rules would wait until a person reads it.

How a person would decide

  • Approve, edit or reject. Each choice would be written to the audit trail.
  • Nothing would pass by rule until the client writes that rule.
  • Every new rule would be replayed on past weeks first, so the client sees what it would have let through.

Never done by NowAI

  • Hold a client's ASAN İmza.
  • Hold or move client money.

We found no rule that lets software file or sign for a company. The accountant files. unknown

  1. Filing through an e-representative, and lending e-signatures (reported): Tax service post on e-representatives

How we plan to start with every client

One week of your work. Two weeks later, your Money Map.

A paid two-week Discovery Sprint on one week of your own data. It would end in a Money Map, with the evidence under every number.
