Site notice

The plain facts about this site: what it is, what it stores and how to read its numbers.

In short

  • No cookies, analytics or tracking
  • Nothing here is an offer or a contract
  • One outside request: the font
  • Search engines are asked not to list it
  1. 01

    What this site is

    This site presents NowAI's plan: the service it will offer, how it will work and the numbers behind it.

    The site names no company entity behind it and no person. Nothing on it is an offer, a quote or a contract.

    Companies, people and amounts in the examples are invented, to show how NowAI would work.

    To get in touch, see How to reach us.

  2. 02

    What this site stores

    Only your language choice, in this browser, under the key nowai-lang.

    No cookies, no analytics, no tracking and no forms. Nothing you do here is sent anywhere.

    To remove the choice, clear this site's data in your browser settings.

  3. 03

    Fonts, and the one outside request

    Your browser fetches the Onest font from Google Fonts. That is the site's only request to another server.

    If the font cannot load, the pages fall back to a system font.

    Source links open government, news and law-firm pages only when you click them. We do not control those pages.

  4. 04

    Not for search engines

    Every page asks search engines not to list it or follow its links (noindex, nofollow).

    That does not make a page private. Anyone with the link can open it.

  5. 05

    What the labels mean

    Every figure on this site carries one label. In one line each:

    • verifiedchecked against an official source.
    • reportedsaid by a source we name; not checked further.
    • estimateour own reasoned figure, not measured.
    • modelfrom NowAI's money model: arithmetic on stated assumptions.
    • targeta goal we have set.
    • syntheticinvented for an example.
    • unknownwe do not know yet.
    • plannedpart of the plan, not in place yet.
    An example of each, in the glossary
  6. 06

    Language drafts

    Azerbaijani and Russian text on this site is a draft for a native speaker to check.

    In the example conversations, each message shows in the page language. A small chip opens the customer's original words.
