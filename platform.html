NowAI Core

Business intelligence that does the work.

Reports tell you what already happened. NowAI Core is designed to read everything that comes into a company, understand it against the company's own records, do the routine work inside the systems it already uses, and bring every decision that matters to the right person, with the evidence. In any sector.

Run a company for a day See it in your sector

01What it does

Four things, in any business.

The seven roles are ways of putting one Core to work. The Core itself is not tied to one task or one industry.

  1. 01

    Reading everything

    Messages, calls, email, documents, photos and system files would all be read, in Azerbaijani and Russian, often mixed in one message. Nothing would be retyped by hand.

  2. 02

    Understanding the company

    One living picture of customers, suppliers, prices, stock, contracts, cash and people, joining up what each department only sees in part.

  3. 03

    Acting within your rules

    Routine work would be done inside the systems the company already uses. Anything risky would wait for a person, with the evidence beside it.

  4. 04

    Telling you what matters

    Instead of reports to dig through, each person in charge would get what changed, what is at risk and what needs their decision, at a time they choose.

04Languages

Azerbaijani and Russian, in text and voice

Customers often mix both languages in one message. NowAI would read and reply in the customer's language.

  • AZ · RU

    Text

    Azerbaijani and Russian, often mixed in one message, in Latin or Cyrillic script.

  • Voice

07What it is not

What NowAI is not

  • Not a chatbot

    A role would finish the job: the order drafted for 1C, the invoice held, the reminder sent.

  • Not a new accounting system

    It would work with your 1C and your bank files. Nothing to migrate.

  • Not an outsourcing agency

    Setup would be configuration, not custom code or a team of people doing the work.

  • Not an IT help desk

    It would not fix laptops, printers or passwords.
