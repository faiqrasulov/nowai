03The Gate

The Gate (the approval queue)

A person would approve anything risky, with the evidence beside it. Otherwise it would not happen.

By rules you approve

  • Orders within a customer's credit limit and the price list.
  • Reminders from approved templates, under rules your finance manager signed off.
  • Small credit notes, under a limit you set.
  • A routine e-qaimə reminder to a supplier, tied to one invoice.

Waits for a person, with the evidence

  • Posting to the books, as a 1C import file.
  • Any payment or payment link. NowAI would only prepare it; a person releases it.
  • Large credit notes and refunds.
  • Orders over a credit limit.
  • Messages outside approved templates.
  • Every new rule, after a replay on past weeks.

Never

  • Hold client money.
  • Hold or use your ASAN İmza.
  • Sign or issue an e-qaimə.
  • Give a role system admin rights.
  • Threaten or pressure a customer.

A rule, in plain words

“Hold any supplier invoice priced above the contract, and draft a polite email to the supplier.”

07What it is not

What NowAI is not

  • Not a chatbot

    A role would finish the job: the order drafted for 1C, the invoice held, the reminder sent.

  • Not a new accounting system

    It would work with your 1C and your bank files. Nothing to migrate.

  • Not an outsourcing agency

    Setup would be configuration, not custom code or a team of people doing the work.

  • Not an IT help desk

    It would not fix laptops, printers or passwords.

How we plan to start with every client

One week of your work. Two weeks later, your Money Map.

A paid two-week Discovery Sprint on one week of your own data. It would end in a Money Map, with the evidence under every number.
