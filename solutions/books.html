02The Gate

What would wait for a person

Books would prepare; the chief accountant would decide. Nothing would reach 1C without a person's click.

By rules you approve

  • Reading statement files and exports
  • Preparing entries, each with its reason
  • Marking clear bank matches for review

Waits for a person, with the evidence

  • Every posting to 1C
  • Every unmatched bank line
  • Any change to an account mapping or rule
  • Corrections to an approved period

Never

  • Post without the chief accountant's click
  • Hold your ASAN İmza
  • File a tax return
  • Move money between accounts

04Hours (shown, not charged)

How its value would be counted

Books would save hours. Hours are shown, not charged: the fee comes only from the four buckets.

    Time saved is real, but hard to prove fairly. So it would never set the fee.

  3. What it would make possible

    Clean, matched books would make the four buckets easier to measure and to check.

06Never done by NowAI

What it would not do

  • It would never post to 1C without the chief accountant's click.
  • It would work with 1C exports and import files, not a live link to 1C.
  • It would never hold your ASAN İmza or file a return.
  • It would not be a new accounting system. Your books stay in 1C.
  • It would not sign as your chief accountant. A person signs.
  • It would not change a closed period. Corrections wait for a person.

07Books

Questions

Would it replace our accountant?

No. It would prepare; your accountant would review, approve and sign. A person stays responsible for the books.

Can it write straight into 1C?

It would prepare 1C import files for a person to post. Whether they load cleanly in your 1C setup would be checked before go-live (unknown until then).

Why are Hours not charged?

Time saved is hard to prove fairly. So Hours are shown in the brief and the statement, and the fee comes only from the four buckets.

Where would our data go?

Only the data you choose, under a written agreement signed before the sprint. Where it is stored would be decided after a lawyer's written answer on data leaving Azerbaijan.
