02The Gate

What would wait for a person

Collections would follow rules the finance manager approved. Anything outside them would wait at the Gate.

By rules you approve

  • Reminders that follow rules the finance manager approved
  • One invoice per reminder, from approved templates
  • Stopping reminders once a payment arrives

Waits for a person, with the evidence

  • Key accounts, for the sales rep
  • Any message outside approved templates
  • Broken promises and long-overdue accounts
  • Disputed invoices

Never

  • Threats, penalties or talk of courts
  • Messages outside the hours you set
  • Take, hold or move money
  • Write off a balance or change payment terms

04Cash in sooner

How its value would be counted

Collections would move two buckets: Cash in sooner and Losses avoided. Both would be counted from your own bank file.

  1. Cash in sooner: the cost of waiting

    Money that arrives earlier would be valued at the cost of waiting for it: 15% a year (estimate).

  2. Losses avoided

    A balance would count as at risk only under a rule your finance manager agrees in the sprint: for example, past an agreed number of days, or over the credit limit. When such a balance is paid, only its expected-loss share would count, set from your own past write-offs. Each payment would count once, in one bucket.

  3. Your own data first

    We found no Azerbaijani data on late payment between firms (unknown), so a sprint would measure your own receivables first.

06Never done by NowAI

What it would not do

  • It would not act as a collection agency. These are polite reminders from your company to its own customers.
  • It would never threaten, add penalties or mention courts.
  • It would never hold money. Payment links only through a licensed provider (planned).
  • It would not write off a balance or change payment terms.
  • Phone calls to late payers would stay with your people.
  • It would not remind a customer who has already paid.

07Collections

Questions

Would reminders annoy good customers?

Paid customers would be skipped, and key accounts would go to their rep. You would set how often reminders go, and at what hours.

Can it send payment links?

Only through a licensed payment provider (planned), once a lawyer confirms the legal route. NowAI would never hold the money.

How would cash in sooner be valued?

As the cost of waiting for money: 15% a year for each day a payment came sooner (estimate). A sprint would measure your own receivables first.

What if it gets something wrong?

Anything risky would wait at the Gate for a person. New rules would be replayed on past weeks first. Each role would start in review-only mode, with every action waiting for approval.
