02The Gate

What would wait for a person

Customer Care would settle small things by your rules. Money, promises and anything clinical would go to a person.

By rules you approve

  • Answers from your price list, stock and policies
  • Credit notes under your limit, with a photo
  • Delivery status from your own records

Waits for a person, with the evidence

  • Credit notes above your limit
  • Refunds and compensation
  • Settling a dispute passed from Collections
  • Any case the customer asks to escalate

Never

  • Medical, clinical or legal advice
  • Promise what your policy does not allow
  • Hide that it is an AI assistant
  • Send or hold money

04Keeps customers (not charged)

How its value would be counted

Customer Care would not be charged. Keeping a customer is real money, but it is hard to measure fairly.

  1. What it would show

    Cases opened, closed and waiting, and credit notes issued. They would appear in the 19:00 brief and the monthly statement.

  2. What would not be counted

    Customers kept would not be priced. No value would be counted twice across roles.

  3. When an answer saves an order

    If a quick answer leads to an order, Sales Desk would count it once, as Extra sales.

The fee would come only from the four buckets: Extra sales, Cash in sooner, Losses avoided and Leaks recovered.

3,500 AZN platform fee a month plus 20% of the value we measure (target).

06Never done by NowAI

What it would not do

  • It would never give medical, clinical or legal advice. Those questions go to a named person.
  • It would never decide a refund or compensation by itself.
  • It would never pretend to be a person.
  • It would not be an IT help desk.
  • It would not handle hard cases alone. It would hand them to a person, with the facts.
  • It would not answer from memory. Every answer would come from your own records.

07Customer Care

Questions

What if a caller wants a person?

It would pass the call or chat to a named person, with a short summary of the case so far.

Would it answer medical questions for clinics or pharmacies?

No. Questions about medicines, doses or symptoms would go to a pharmacist, doctor or nurse. It would handle bookings, prices and practical questions only.

Which languages would it use?

Azerbaijani and Russian, in text and voice, often mixed in one message (planned). Azerbaijani voice is the hardest part, so anything unclear would wait at the Gate for a person.

What if it gets something wrong?

Anything risky would wait at the Gate for a person. New rules would be replayed on past weeks first. Each role would start in review-only mode, with every action waiting for approval.
