One shared NowAI Core

The roles share channels, company records, rules and approvals. Each workflow is configured for the work it handles.

  1. Channels

    WhatsApp Business, email, voice and web, landing in one place.

  2. Input processing

    Documents, photos and voice notes in Azerbaijani and Russian.

  3. Company memory

    Customers, suppliers, prices, contracts and your rules.

  4. Connectors

    1C exports, bank statements and e-qaimə files.

  5. The Gate (the approval queue)

    Anything risky waits for a person, with its evidence.

  6. Measurement

    Track financial outcomes with the evidence behind each figure.

Choosing the first roles

Choose the roles for your first deployment

The Discovery Sprint identifies the workflows with the strongest evidence of value.

  1. 01

    One week of your data

    Under a written agreement, the sprint reads one week of orders, invoices, calls and payments.

  2. 02

    A Money Map

    The Money Map links delays, avoidable costs and financial opportunities to the underlying records.

  3. 03

    Role selection

    Select one or two roles for the highest-value workflows. Add roles as new workflows are assessed.

  4. 04

    Replay and review-only mode

    The roles run on your past weeks first. Then they go live, with every action waiting at the Gate.

Approval rules and limits
  • Act as an IT help desk, or hold admin rights in your systems.
  • Decide insurance claims, credit or medical questions. A licensed or named person decides.
  • Hold client money or your ASAN İmza. Payment links only through a licensed provider.
  • Write first to a customer who has not opted in, or outside approved templates.
  • Pretend to be a person. The first reply in every conversation says it is an AI assistant.
  • Cut jobs. The aim is growth without new hires, not layoffs.
