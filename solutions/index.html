04How a client would pick its first roles

A client would start with the one or two roles that hold the most money.

The Discovery Sprint would show which ones. The platform fee would cover up to two roles (target).

  1. 01

    One week of your data

    Under a written agreement, the sprint would read one week of orders, invoices, calls and payments.

  2. 02

    A Money Map

    It would show where work waits and money leaks, by bucket, with evidence under every number.

  3. 03

    The first one or two roles

    You would start with the roles behind the biggest buckets. Others could follow later.

  4. 04

    Replay, then review-only

    The roles would run on your past weeks first. Then they would go live, with every action waiting at the Gate.

05Never done by NowAI

What no role would do.

The same limits would hold for all seven roles, in every industry.

  • Act as an IT help desk, or hold admin rights in your systems.
  • Decide insurance claims, credit or medical questions. A licensed or named person decides.
  • Hold client money or your ASAN İmza. Payment links only through a licensed provider (planned).
  • Write first to a customer who has not opted in, or outside approved templates.
  • Pretend to be a person. The first reply in every conversation would say it is an AI assistant.
  • Cut jobs. The aim is growth without new hires, not layoffs.

How we plan to start with every client

One week of your work. Two weeks later, your Money Map.

A paid two-week Discovery Sprint on one week of your own data. It would end in a Money Map, with the evidence under every number.
