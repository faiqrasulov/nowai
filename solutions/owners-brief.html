02The Gate

What waits for a person

The brief reports and asks. It never approves its own proposals.

By rules you approve

  • Sending the brief at the hour you choose
  • Reporting what the other roles did today
  • Adding the week's measured value each Friday

Waits for a person, with the evidence

  • Every decision in the brief, for you
  • An order or credit it reports but cannot approve
  • Any change to who receives the brief

Never

  • Decide for you
  • Approve its own proposals
  • Send the brief to anyone you did not name
  • Report a number without its evidence

06Never done by NowAI

What it does not do

  • It is not a dashboard to maintain. There is no new app to open.
  • It never decides for the owner. It reports and asks.
  • It does not show a number it cannot back with evidence.
  • It does not replace the monthly statement of measured value.
  • It does not go to anyone the owner did not name.
  • It does not flood anyone with alerts. Each person gets only what they chose to see.

07Owner's Brief

Questions

Can I change the time?

Yes. Each person who receives a brief chooses their own pace: once a day, at set hours, or as things happen. The 19:00 in the story is only that owner's choice.

Can I reply to the brief?

Yes. A short reply such as "ask for part payment" is carried out and recorded with your name.

What if the day was quiet?

The brief says so in one line. It does not invent news.

Which languages does it use?

Azerbaijani and Russian, in text and voice, often mixed in one message. Anything unclear waits at the Gate for a person.
