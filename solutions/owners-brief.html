02The Gate

What would wait for a person

The brief would report and ask. It would never approve its own proposals.

By rules you approve

  • Sending the brief at the hour you choose
  • Reporting what the other roles did today
  • Adding the week's measured value each Friday

Waits for a person, with the evidence

  • Every decision in the brief, for you
  • An order or credit it reports but cannot approve
  • Any change to who receives the brief

Never

  • Decide for you
  • Approve its own proposals
  • Send the brief to anyone you did not name
  • Report a number without its evidence

04Executive reporting included

How its value would be counted

Owner's Brief would not be charged. It is the reason an owner would keep NowAI: one view of the day, every evening.

  1. Not charged

    The brief moves no bucket by itself. It shows what the other roles moved.

  2. The same figures as the fee

    Money found this week would use the same measured value, with the same evidence, as the monthly statement.

  3. Why it matters

    An owner who sees the money each evening can judge the fee each month.

Hours would appear in the brief too, always marked shown, not charged.

06Never done by NowAI

What it would not do

  • It would not be a dashboard to maintain. There is no new app to open.
  • It would never decide for the owner. It reports and asks.
  • It would not show a number it cannot back with evidence.
  • It would not replace the monthly statement of measured value.
  • It would not go to anyone the owner did not name.
  • It would not fill the evening with alerts. One message, not a stream.

07Owner's Brief

Questions

Can I change the time?

Yes. 19:00 is the story's hour. You would choose the hour and who receives it.

Can I reply to the brief?

Yes. A short reply such as "ask for part payment" would be carried out and recorded with your name.

What if the day was quiet?

The brief would say so in one line. It would not invent news.

Which languages would it use?

Azerbaijani and Russian, in text and voice, often mixed in one message (planned). Azerbaijani voice is the hardest part, so anything unclear would wait at the Gate for a person.
