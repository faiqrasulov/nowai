06Never done by NowAI

What it does not do

  • It never pays a supplier or moves money.
  • It never signs or issues an e-qaimə, and never holds your ASAN İmza.
  • It does not accept or admit a debt in a reconciliation act.
  • It does not argue with suppliers. Every email waits for purchasing.
  • It does not post to 1C. Books prepares entries, and the chief accountant approves.
  • It does not change a contract price. Purchasing updates prices.

07Payables Check

Questions

What if the contract price changed?

It checks against the contract on file. If a new price was agreed, purchasing updates it once, and the hold clears.

Does it need an e-qaimə API?

No. It starts from the e-qaimə files you export, so it needs no live link to the e-qaimə system.

Will suppliers take offence?

Every email is polite, tied to one invoice, and approved by purchasing first. In the story the supplier is not a villain: its billing slipped, and nobody was checking.

Where does our data go?

Only the data you choose, under a written agreement signed before the sprint. Where it is stored is set out in that agreement.
