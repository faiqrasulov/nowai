People Desk would answer and prepare. Every decision about a person's job or pay would stay with a person.

Waits for a person, with the evidence

  • Every leave approval, for the manager

Never

06Never done by NowAI

What it would not do

  • It would never decide who is hired, promoted or dismissed.
  • Pay disputes, discipline and dismissals would go to a named HR person, never to NowAI.
  • It would never enter or sign anything in EMAS. A person does.
  • Staff data is personal data under Law No. 998-IIIQ. Sending it abroad would need a lawyer's answer first.
  • It would not screen candidates in the first plan. Hiring help is an idea only.
  • It would not give legal advice on labour law. It would quote your policy and pass the rest to HR.

07People Desk

Questions

Which staff would use it?

Mostly staff without a desk: drivers, cashiers, warehouse staff and pharmacists. They would ask on WhatsApp, in Azerbaijani or Russian.

Can it file contracts in EMAS?

No. No EMAS API was found. It would prepare the data; a person would enter and sign it.

Is this an HR system?

No. Your records stay in 1C and your HR files. People Desk would answer, prepare and remind; people decide.

Where would our data go?

Only the data you choose, under a written agreement signed before the sprint. Where it is stored would be decided after a lawyer's written answer on data leaving Azerbaijan.
