02The Gate

What waits for a person

Sales Desk acts alone only inside rules you approve. Everything else waits at the Gate, with its evidence.

By rules you approve

  • Orders within the customer's credit limit and your stock
  • Quotes at list price
  • Replies inside a chat the customer started

Waits for a person, with the evidence

  • Orders over a credit limit, for the finance manager
  • Prices or discounts outside the list
  • Any message outside approved templates
  • Orders for goods that are out of stock

Never

  • Set prices or negotiate
  • Change a customer's credit limit
  • Write first to a customer who has not opted in
  • Take or hold money

04Extra sales

How its value is counted

Sales Desk moves one bucket: Extra sales, counted at margin. Only orders that would otherwise have been lost count.

  1. Which orders count

    Only orders above your usual after-hours and busy-line baseline count. The sprint sets that baseline from your own data, and you agree it in writing.

  2. At their margin

    Each order counts at its margin, not at its full price.

  3. With evidence

    The monthly statement lists each counted order with the message it came from. You can check every line.

06Never done by NowAI

What it does not do

  • No separate ordering app. Customers keep writing where they already write.
  • It never sets prices or negotiates. Exceptions go to a sales manager.
  • It never changes a credit limit. The finance manager owns limits.
  • It does not write first to customers who have not opted in.
  • It does not take key accounts away from your sales reps.
  • It does not guess. An unclear order is asked back in the same chat.

07Sales Desk

Questions

What if a voice note is unclear?

It asks the customer back in the same chat. It does not guess a product or a quantity.

Do customers know they are writing to an AI?

Yes. The first reply says it is the company's AI assistant.

Does it write orders straight into 1C?

Not at first. It prepares a 1C import file for a person to post. We check that this route works in your own 1C setup before anything goes live.

Which languages does it use?

Azerbaijani and Russian, in text and voice, often mixed in one message. Anything unclear waits at the Gate for a person.
