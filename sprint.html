01How the sprint would run

Three steps, two weeks

Every sprint would follow the same plan (target).

  1. 01

    Choose a week, under a written agreement

    You pick the week and the doors: WhatsApp exports, an inbox, a 1C export, bank statements.

  2. 02

    We would read it in Azerbaijani and Russian

    Chats, voice notes, invoices, delivery notes and exports, line by line.

  3. 03

    We would bring the Money Map

    Where work waits and money leaks, in AZN, with evidence under every number.

  1. Day 1

    Agreement and data

    Sign the data agreement, choose a representative sample and check for promotions, holidays and unusual payment cycles before estimating monthly value.

  2. Days 2-5

    Reading and matching

    Every message, invoice and payment read and matched.

  3. Days 6-8

    Findings checked

    First findings checked with your team, so nothing is a surprise.

  4. Days 9-10

    The Money Map

    Presented to the owner, with evidence.

Two roles would then run on your week in replay. You keep the Money Map either way.

06Design partners

Three design-partner places, free or at a discount.

The first three sprints will go to design partners (target). Places open once the data agreement and a lawyer's written answer on client data are ready.

  1. 01Design-partner placePlanned, not open yet

What a partner would give

  • One week of data, under a written agreement.
  • Time from the owner or finance director, to check the findings.
  • Honest feedback on the Money Map, good or bad.

What a partner would get

  • A Money Map of its own week, free or at a discount.
  • The first two roles in replay on its past weeks, at two of the three partners (target).
  • A say in how the roles and the Gate would work.

Who would not be a fit

  • Micro firms and small online-only shops: the price is too high for them.
  • Large banks, large telecom operators and state bodies.
  • Firms that cannot share one week of data under a written agreement.
  • Anyone who wants a chatbot only.

07Questions

Before you ask for a sprint

Where would our data go?

Only the data you choose, under a written agreement signed before the sprint. Where it is stored would be decided after a lawyer's written answer on data leaving Azerbaijan.

What if it gets something wrong?

Anything risky would wait at the Gate for a person. New rules would be replayed on past weeks first. Each role would start in review-only mode, with every action waiting for approval.

How exactly would the fee be worked out?

Each month: the 3,500 AZN platform fee plus 20% of the value measured that month in the four buckets (target). Hours are shown, not charged. How each bucket would be counted

What are Hours?

Staff time the roles would save, written in AZN. Hours appear in the brief and in value ÷ fee. No fee is charged on them.

Would NowAI take payments?

No. NowAI would never hold money. Payment links would run only through a licensed provider (planned; the legal route is confirmed first).

What if the Money Map shows too little?

Then we would say so. The price would be set from your measured map, or we would tell you it does not pay. You keep the Money Map either way.
