The company in this story

21:47

Wednesday night · Sales Desk · Extra sales

Bulaq Market ordered at 21:47. It was in 1C by morning.

Bulaq Market in Yasamal sends a voice note: 20 boxes of sunflower oil, 10 of pasta. Nobody at Aventa Group is at work.

Sales Desk checks stock in 1C, finds room on Route 4 and checks the shop's credit: 4,200 AZN, 45 days late, still within its limit. It replies in Azerbaijani with the total, 1,248.00 AZN. The draft order is in 1C before Kamran, the morning dispatcher, arrives.

Rule 12: within the limit, it goes through. Over the limit, it waits for Səbinə.

Every first reply says it is the company's AI assistant. Payment links in reminders go through a licensed payment provider, and the legal route is confirmed first.

In the story, the draft goes straight into 1C. At a client, NowAI prepares a 1C import file for a person to post.

09:15

Payables Check · Leaks recovered

4.20 in the contract. 4.50 on the invoice. Held.

Barxana Konserv emails 14 invoices. The warehouse sends two photos of paper delivery notes on WhatsApp. Payables Check matches every invoice to the order, the delivery, the contract price and the e-qaimə. Twelve match.

Tomato paste does not: 3,800 jars billed at 4.50 against 4.20 in the contract. That is 1,140 AZN, 7.1% over. The invoice is held, and an email to the supplier waits for Elçin. BK-0417, sugar, has no e-qaimə yet, so 612.00 AZN of ƏDV is at risk, and the supplier is reminded. Leyla approves the twelve clean entries in one batch.

Barxana Konserv is not a villain. Its billing slipped, and nobody was checking.

11:02

Customer Care · Customers kept

Gülüstan Market calls in Russian. The eggs arrived broken.

Customer Care answers at once. It sees the 08:40 delivery, signed at the shop, and asks for a photo on WhatsApp. The photo arrives during the call.

A credit note for 3 × 18.60 = 55.80 AZN goes out by rule, because it is under 100 AZN. The eggs are replaced on the next delivery, and the claim is logged against the packer. The call takes 2 minutes 40 seconds.

Gülüstan Market · 1 of 3

Under 100 AZN: by rule. Bigger credit notes wait for a person.

Not every job finds money. Credit notes paid out count for nothing in our fee.

Callers always hear that they are talking to an AI. A lawyer's written answer on AI voice calls and call recording comes before any call goes live.

Mon → Thu

Collections · Cash in sooner · Losses avoided

Late payers hear from you every day, politely, in their language.

On Monday at 10:00, 214 shops owe Aventa Group 1,198,600 AZN past their terms. Collections sorts them by risk. 180 get a reminder in their own language, under rules Səbinə approved. 34 key accounts stay with their sales reps, with the message drafted. Gülüstan Market is one of them: 13,800 AZN, 62 days, on a 4,000 AZN limit.

Through the week the replies come in. Tənək Market pays 9,240 AZN. Nərgiz Market pays 3,960. Bulaq Market promises Friday. Səhər Market says the goods were damaged, so its case goes to Customer Care. By Thursday, 41,200 AZN is in. Overdue is down to 1,157,400 AZN.

Gülüstan Market · 2 of 3

Polite reminders from the company to its own customers. No threats, no collectors.

Cash collected is not our fee. Only the cost of waiting for it counts.

For a distributor this is the biggest bucket and the least proven. A sprint measures it first.

19:00

Owner's Brief · Thursday

Rauf gets one message. Only one thing needs him.

Everything else went to the people whose job it is. Leyla approved 12 entries. Elçin approved an email. Səbinə's rules sent 180 reminders.

Over-limit orders wait for Səbinə. This one is 9,800 AZN over, so she left it to Rauf. The message matches the brief that built up as you scrolled, line for line.

Gülüstan Market · 3 of 3

What NowAI knew about Gülüstan Market at 19:00: owes 13,800 AZN, 62 days. Limit 4,000. Broken eggs at 11:02, credit note sent, case closed. Held for its sales rep this week.

Your call. Tonight you are Rauf.

Gülüstan Market owes 13,800 AZN, 62 days late, on a 4,000 AZN limit. It wants another 2,400 AZN of goods.

Approved. The 2,400 AZN order goes on tomorrow's route. In NowAI, this click is logged in the audit trail with Rauf's name.

Held. The order stays off tomorrow's route. The sales rep gets a note to call in the morning.

Asked. NowAI asks Gülüstan Market for 5,000 AZN before tomorrow's delivery.

Rauf asked for part payment, as the film shows. None of the three is wrong. In NowAI, each is logged with who decided.

Later

Later that week · Payables Check · Leaks recovered

Third time in six weeks. It suggests a rule, and tests it first.

Barxana Konserv has billed above contract three times: 1,140 AZN this week, 780 three weeks ago, 390 five weeks ago. NowAI writes a rule in plain words: hold every Barxana Konserv invoice for a price check before payment.

It replays the rule on the last six weeks. It would have caught 2,310 AZN. Elçin reads it and approves. It becomes Rule 15.

Nothing changes until a rule has been replayed and a person says yes. Managers can read, change or switch off any rule.

The Gate (the approval queue)

Nothing risky moves until a person clicks Approve.

Does on its own, by rules you approve

  • Confirms orders within a shop's credit limit.
  • Sends reminders under rules your finance manager approved.
  • Sends credit notes under 100 AZN.
  • Matches invoices to the order, delivery note, contract price and e-qaimə.

Waits for a person, with the evidence

  • Posting to the books.
  • Sending money.
  • Large credit notes.
  • Orders over a credit limit.
  • Messages to key accounts.
  • Any message outside your rules.
  • Every new rule, after a replay on past weeks.

Never

  • Holds your money.
  • Holds your ASAN İmza.
  • Threatens or pressures your customers.
  • Changes a rule without a person's yes.
