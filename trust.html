01Commitments

Five promises to every client

They will hold from the first sprint, before any role goes live.

  • A signed data agreement before NowAI reads anything.
  • A person approves every risky action, with the evidence beside it.
  • Every action recorded: what happened, on which evidence, and who approved it.
  • NowAI will never hold your ASAN İmza, your money or admin rights.
  • A lawyer's written answer on data and calls before the first sprint.

02Control design

The part that proposes is not the part that approves.

A role would propose an action. Rules written with you, or a named person, would decide whether it goes ahead.

  1. 1 · ProposeThe role drafts the order, the hold or the reminder.
  2. 2 · Check the rulesRules you approved decide if it may pass alone.
  3. 3 · The GateAnything else waits for a person, with the evidence.
  4. 4 · RecordEvery step is written to the audit trail.

  • Proposals and approvals kept apart

    The model that drafts an action would not be the thing that approves it. Approval would come from a rule or a person.

  • Permissions that expire

    Each role's access would be limited in time and renewed on purpose (design).

  • Evidence beside every item

    The invoice line, the contract price, the customer's balance: the person approving would see why it waits.

03Data agreement

A written data agreement comes before any sprint.

You choose the data. Nothing else would be read.

target What every data agreement will cover.

Which data
Only the doors and the week you pick: for example one WhatsApp export, one inbox, one 1C export.
Who sees it
Named people on both sides, listed in the agreement.
Where it is stored
Named in the agreement, once hosting is chosen.
How long it is kept
For the sprint and the time you agree, no longer.
How it ends
Returned or deleted, as you choose, with a written note that it was done.

05Access

What NowAI would never hold

Some things stay with your people, always. These are design rules, not features to switch on.

  • Never

    ASAN İmza

    NowAI would never hold or use your ASAN İmza. Signing and filing stay with your people.

  • Never

    Client money

    NowAI would never hold money. Payment links only through a licensed provider (planned; the legal route is confirmed first).

  • Never

    Admin rights

    Roles would not get system admin rights. Each would get only the access its job needs.

06Hosting and the law

Hosting will follow the law.

Where client data lives will be decided after a lawyer's written answer, not before.

  • unknown

    The options

    In-country hosting is one option. Each will be priced and compared before the first sprint.

  • reported

    Personal data law

    Personal data falls under Law No. 998-IIIQ. Sending it abroad may need consent. A lawyer's written answer comes before the first sprint.

  • estimate

    Sensitive data

    Health, staff and children's data need extra care, and may need extra rules.

07WhatsApp and calls

Customers would always know they are talking to an AI.

Messages and calls carry the most risk of surprise. So the rules here are strict.

  • Messages a business starts need the customer's opt-in and an approved template. Reminders must be tied to one item. reported

  • Replies inside a conversation the customer started would not need a template.

  • Whether callers must be told they speak to an AI, and that calls are recorded, is an open legal question. NowAI would say it anyway: a real reply would always open with the AI line. Where an example on this site leaves it out, the page says so. unknown

How every reply would open (draft wording)

To a customer writing in Azerbaijani

Bu, şirkətin süni intellekt köməkçisidir.

To a customer writing in Russian

Это ИИ-ассистент компании.

Both lines mean: “This is the company's AI assistant.”

Each reply would open in the customer's language. Both lines are draft wording, for a lawyer and a native speaker to check.
